Q+A Katha - Courage 🧠

Question: Who was the first man to travel into outer space twice?

Answer: Gus Grissom - History. Itha kelvi Courage pathi - Answer Gus Grissom. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Courage nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Courage oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Courage pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Courage ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Courage nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Courage ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
